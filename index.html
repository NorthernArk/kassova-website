Kassova

One governed place for a family office to run its money: control, consolidation, treasury, planning, assets, records, workflow, risk and data, instead of side spreadsheets.

What Kassova does

  • Control and governance

    Maker-checker approval on every entry, a controlled month close and a full audit trail of who did what and when.

  • Consolidation

    A double-entry ledger for every entity a family holds, kept separate per family and rolled up into one view.

  • Treasury and liquidity

    Cash positions across entities and accounts, upcoming obligations and the liquidity to meet them.

  • Planning and performance

    Budgets and plans set against actuals, with plan-versus-actual reports by entity and period.

  • Family-office assets

    A register of holdings, from securities to property and private investments, each with its own history.

  • Documents and records

    Statements, agreements and notices filed against the entities they belong to, kept and retired on a defined lifecycle.

  • Workflow, people and relationships

    Tasks, approvals and the people and relationships around each family, in one place.

  • Risk and regulatory registers

    Risks, obligations and regulatory items held as registers with owners, reviews and an audit trail.

  • Data and integrations

    Bank, custodian, payments and payroll data arrive on schedule, and every feed shows its last success, so no failure goes unseen.

  • Family experience and AI governance

    Clear reporting for the families served, and governed, reviewable use of AI assistance.

  • Security and scale

    Each family's data is isolated at the database, access is private and reviewed, and the system is load-tested before it grows.

How it is built

  • Every number traces back to a ledger entry, and every entry to the person who made and approved it.
  • Each family's books are kept apart; nothing is shared between them.
  • Private by design: Kassova is not reachable from the public internet.
